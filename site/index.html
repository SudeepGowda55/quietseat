In the call. Without the sound.

Quiet Seat captions a Discord voice call on Even Realities G2 glasses, only for the people who said yes. When someone asks you something by name, the glasses show the question and three replies. Tap one on the R1 ring and it is posted in the call's chat.

Watch the recorded callHow it works

Consent first

Only the people who pressed Caption me.

The bot joins the call with you and posts a card. Whoever presses Caption me is captioned. Everyone else appears as someone speaking, never as words, and leaving the call ends their consent.

Captions

Their words on your glasses, about half a second later.

Speech becomes text on your own laptop with whisper.cpp (small.en), not on a server. The G2 shows about 54 characters by 9 lines, so the newest lines stay in view.

Your name

Asked by name, the glasses switch to the question.

Whisper often writes Sudeep as “Sudhi” or “Sudip”, and the detector still matches. A question about you (“Did Sudeep push the fix?”) stays a caption. A 1.5B language model on the laptop drafts three replies.

One tap

Scroll with the ring, tap, and it's in the chat.

The reply is posted in the call's text chat, addressed to the person who asked. The call carries on without anyone waiting for you to unmute.
